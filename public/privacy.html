Privacy Policy

AWAAS Command Center · Last updated: 14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AWAAS Services Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in AWAAS Command Center ("the Service"), a marketing operations platform that connects to Meta (Facebook and Instagram) on behalf of businesses that link their advertising accounts and Pages to the Service.

Who we are

The Service is operated by AWAAS Services Pvt. Ltd., N5/237, IRC Village, Nayapalli, Bhubaneswar - 751015, Odisha, India. Contact: contact@awaas.world.

Information we collect

  • Advertising performance data from Meta. When a business connects its Meta ad account, we receive campaign, ad set, and ad configuration and performance metrics — such as impressions, clicks, spend, and cost per lead — together with the associated campaign, ad set, ad, and creative identifiers.
  • Page & account information. The list of Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts a connecting user manages, basic Page details, and access tokens used solely to perform the operations the business has authorized.
  • Content published through the Service. Posts and advertisements a business creates and explicitly approves for publishing through the Service, and the identifiers Meta returns for that published content.
  • Command Center account data. Names, email addresses, and roles of the marketing-team users who log in to the Service, and the campaign activity they record.

The Service does not receive lead form submissions or any personal information about individuals who respond to a business's advertisements. Lead enquiries are handled separately by AWAAS CRM under its own privacy policy; the Service holds only anonymous internal reference identifiers and advertising identifiers for attribution.

How we use information

  • To display and analyze each connected business's own advertising performance so its marketing team can evaluate and improve campaigns.
  • To publish content to the business's own Facebook Page, Instagram account, or ad account — only when a user of that business explicitly approves each publish action.
  • To pause, resume, or adjust the business's own campaigns when a user of that business explicitly approves each change.
  • To attribute advertising outcomes to campaigns and creatives, so the Service can suggest improvements to the business's future advertising.
  • To operate, secure, and support the Service.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use data received from Meta for advertising our own services or for any purpose other than serving the business that owns it.

Meta Platform data & permissions

The Service uses the following Meta permissions strictly for the purposes below:

  • ads_read — to fetch the connected ad account's campaign and ad performance metrics for display and analysis.
  • ads_management — to create, pause, resume, or adjust campaigns and ads on the connected ad account, each action individually approved by a user of that business.
  • pages_show_list — to show the connecting user the list of Pages they manage, so they can choose which to connect.
  • pages_read_engagement — to read basic details of the connected Page.
  • pages_manage_posts — to publish posts the business has approved to its own connected Page.
  • instagram_basic — to identify the Instagram account linked to the connected Page.
  • instagram_content_publish — to publish content the business has approved to its own connected Instagram account.
  • business_management — to identify the business that owns the connected assets.

Our use of information received from Meta APIs adheres to the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

Access tokens

Access tokens are stored encrypted on our servers, are never exposed in the browser, and are used solely for the authorized operations described above. A business can disconnect its ad account or Page at any time, which removes the stored token and stops any further data collection.

Where data is stored & sub-processors

  • Supabase (database, authentication, and storage hosting; Mumbai, India region) — stores advertising performance and account data.
  • Anthropic — AI assistance features process campaign context to generate marketing suggestions; no data is used to train third-party models.
  • OpenAI — AI image-generation features process creative briefs to produce advertising imagery; no data is used to train third-party models.

Data retention

Advertising performance and account data is retained for as long as the connected business uses the Service, or until deletion is requested. Access tokens are deleted when a business disconnects its ad account or Page.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing contact@awaas.world with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and the email address associated with your account. We will delete the associated records within 30 days and confirm once done. A business can also disconnect its Meta assets at any time, which removes its stored access tokens and stops any further data collection.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email above. For grievances under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may write to our Grievance Officer at contact@awaas.world; we aim to acknowledge grievances within 72 hours and resolve them within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision.
